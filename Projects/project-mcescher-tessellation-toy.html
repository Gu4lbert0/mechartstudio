M.C. Escher Tessellation toy

An interactive geometric toy that encourages creative exploration of repeating shapes, interlocking patterns, and tessellated designs inspired by the work of M. C. Escher.

Back to Projects

Project Summary

The M.C. Escher Tessellation toy is a hands-on design project centered on the geometry of tessellation. Its interlocking pieces can be arranged into repeating patterns, giving users a playful way to investigate symmetry, spatial relationships, and pattern-making.

The project combines mathematical concepts with physical product design to create an approachable creative experience. Additional development details, photographs, and demonstrations will be added as the project progresses.

Project Focus

  • Exploration of tessellating geometry and repeating patterns.
  • Development of interlocking shapes for hands-on play.
  • Application of symmetry and spatial-design principles.
  • Prototype refinement for an engaging user experience.
