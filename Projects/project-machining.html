Project Summary

The CNC Machining project showcases components manufactured for a variety of engineering and robotics applications. Each part demonstrates practical machining experience, from interpreting engineering drawings and preparing CAM toolpaths to producing finished components that meet functional and dimensional requirements.The collection highlights a range of machining operations, materials, and manufacturing techniques used to support prototype development and mechanical system integration across multiple projects.

My Role

  • Interpreted engineering drawings and CAD models to manufacture functional mechanical components.
  • Developed CAM toolpaths and machining strategies for a variety of part geometries.
  • Manufactured precision components used in robotics, automation, and prototype development.
  • Performed dimensional inspection and iterative refinements to achieve required tolerances.
  • Continuously improved machining techniques through hands-on manufacturing experience.
